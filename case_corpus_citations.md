## Critic warnings

- Therapy timing reported in only 4 of 6 records; any timing comparison is incomplete.
- Corpus includes 1 record(s) labeled early therapy and 3 labeled delayed — descriptive counts only; not a controlled comparison.
- Neurologic outcomes are incompletely reported; recovery comparisons are limited.
- Case reports cannot prove that earlier therapy causes better neurologic recovery.

## Evidence gaps

- Therapy timing not reported in 2 of 6 records (abstract-limited).
- Neurologic outcome not reported in 1 of 6 records.
- Symptoms-to-diagnosis interval not reported in 3 of 6 records.
- Diagnosis-to-treatment interval not reported in 3 of 6 records.
- Abstract-only extraction may miss timing and outcome details present only in full text.
- Case reports and small series cannot establish population-level causation.
