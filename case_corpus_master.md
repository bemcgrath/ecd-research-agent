## Critic warnings

- Therapy timing reported in only 5 of 7 unique records; any timing comparison is incomplete.
- Neurologic outcomes are incompletely reported; recovery comparisons are limited.
- 2 review/large-series row(s) are separated from single-case tallies — do not treat large n as one patient.
- Case reports cannot prove that earlier therapy causes better neurologic recovery.

## Evidence gaps

- Therapy timing not reported in 2 of 7 unique single-case/small-series records (abstract-limited).
- Neurologic outcome not reported in 1 of 7 unique single-case/small-series records.
- Symptoms-to-diagnosis interval not reported in 1 of 7 unique records.
- Diagnosis-to-treatment interval not reported in 4 of 7 unique records.
- CNS involvement unclear or not reported in 1 of 7 unique records.
- 2 review/large-series row(s) listed separately; not mixed into unique single-case timing counts.
- Abstract-only extraction may miss timing and outcome details present only in full text.
- Case reports and small series cannot establish population-level causation.
